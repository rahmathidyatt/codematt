## Overview

Code Reader is a web project for listening to PDF and DOCX documents. The idea is simple: give readers a way to listen, pause, and continue through a document at their own pace.

> **Content status:** This is a foundation case study based on the owner's project brief. Product behavior, screenshots, live deployment, and results have not been independently verified for this portfolio. The date above is the case study entry date, not a product launch date.

## The problem

Reading a long document on a screen is not always the most convenient way to take in its contents. A speech interface offers another way to work through the text.

## Project direction

The brief calls for PDF and DOCX input, text preview, and playback controls. It also describes page-range selection and multilingual reading using voices available through the browser or device.

## Technical approach

The project uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Speech depends on browser and device capabilities, so voice availability can differ between users.
