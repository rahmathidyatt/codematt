## Problem

Building agent schedules manually requires repeated checks: shift allocation, holidays, duplicate names on a day, and readability.

## Solution

Floor Time Scheduler prepares a month of Monday-to-Saturday morning and afternoon shifts. Agent data can be imported from CSV or Excel.

## Implementation focus

- Avoid assigning a person twice on the same day.
- Exclude configured holidays.
- Set weekly frequency limits and distribute shifts as evenly as schedule capacity allows.
- Produce image, PDF, and Excel outputs for operational use.

## Presentation decisions

The schedule prioritizes larger text, adequate spacing, and names that do not get clipped. Readability matters more than decoration, especially for an audience spanning age groups.

## Challenges

Randomization should reduce repeated weekday patterns across weeks. Fair allocation must also account for the number of agents and available slots, which can constrain possible combinations.
