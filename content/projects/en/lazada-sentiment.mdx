## Question

How can app reviews be grouped into positive, neutral, and negative sentiment?

## Data and labels

Reviews come from Google Play through `google-play-scraper`. Labels are mapped from ratings: one or two stars are negative, three stars neutral, and four or five stars positive.

## Text processing

Indonesian text is cleaned and processed with Sastrawi. Text representations are then used for K-Nearest Neighbors classification.

## Considerations

A rating does not always align with the written review. Mapping ratings to labels is a practical approach, but it is not a substitute for human sentiment annotation.

## Result limitations

This case study does not yet include the notebook, data split, and reproducible evaluation report. It therefore does not present an accuracy figure as a verified final result.
