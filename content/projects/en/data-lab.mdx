## Goal

Data Lab brings together the first steps of working with a dataset: opening a file, understanding its structure, and spotting quality issues before deeper analysis.

## Foundation

Phase 1 focuses on CSV and Excel uploads, sheet selection, previews, column-type inspection, missing values, duplicates, and internal row identities.

## Portable sessions

Permanent server history is not a foundation requirement. Instead, session checkpoints can be exported and imported so work remains portable.

## Security decisions

Checkpoint archives need bounds on file sizes, member counts, and total decompressed data. Paths escaping the destination, symlinks, and invalid archive members must be rejected.

## Status

The foundation has been reported as working. Further modules are being developed incrementally. This portfolio does not publish user-uploaded data or claim permanent session storage.
